# Split Points

## MELA

- PitchWheel uses the final `Singu` / `BOTHFINAL` PitchWheel split points.
- Final throttle merges old low and old med into final low.
- Combined PitchWheel+Throttle is formed by putting separate final PitchWheel and Throttle labels together.

## Baseline

- PitchWheel low: `Pwheel <= -10`
- PitchWheel med: `-10 < Pwheel <= 10`
- PitchWheel high: `Pwheel > 10`
- Throttle low: `Throttle <= 0.5`
- Throttle high: `Throttle > 0.5`
- Combined PitchWheel+Throttle is formed by putting separate baseline PitchWheel and Throttle labels together.
